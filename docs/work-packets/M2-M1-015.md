## Acceptance criteria

1. Vehicle registration is represented as three household vehicle-registration planning obligations in total. Existing historical registration-like economic events remain evidence and are not duplicated into new spend events. Unknown vehicle identity, future amount, and due date remain unknown rather than invented.
2. The prior duplicate network reserve is reconciled so only the customer-confirmed network-switch reserve remains in active checking earmarks. Historical correction is preserved without mutating provider-observed balances.
3. Allowance balances are canonical MIRROR planning/allocation state and purchase debits become active. A spend row only debits an allowance when its effective allowance classification is `YES` and the debit has a deterministic canonical event identity.
4. Allowance debit is idempotent: replay/recalculation cannot double-debit the same economic event.
5. Refunds/credits and reclassification are reversible and reconcile back to the originating allowance event where evidence permits. Ambiguous refunds do not silently credit an allowance.
6. Split purchases are not guessed. Until explicit split attribution exists, one canonical event has one effective allowance treatment; later split support must preserve economic-event total reconciliation.
7. Existing category, necessity, allowance, vendor and review-note resolution continues to live on the canonical FinOps Ledger rows keyed by stable event identity. Spending Dropdowns remains an input/view surface only.
8. Pig Phet/shared-budget evidence remains provenance-bound and may corroborate or add canonical obligations/plans, but must not overwrite provider facts or create duplicates. A human-readable comparison projection may show the source budget alongside canonical/current actuals.
9. Receipt/order/photo/serial/warranty/product evidence must join the same MIRROR entity/evidence/relation graph. This packet records any missing generalized capability as follow-on work rather than creating an isolated receipt or asset database.
10. Private-live readback verifies corrected earmarks, three registration plan items, allowance debit state, and no spreadsheet formula errors in touched ranges.
11. No private financial values, account identifiers, transaction identifiers, receipts, or household operational details are committed to public Git.
