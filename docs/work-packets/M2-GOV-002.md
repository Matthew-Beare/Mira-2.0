## Required behavior

1. Every materially new product idea, feature, workflow, requirement, behavior, or implementation-discovered capability is audited against `FEATURES.md` and `BACKLOG.md`.
2. Existing canonical coverage is recorded by stable feature/work IDs rather than duplicated.
3. Novel ideas receive a stable semantic feature ID and dependency-ranked backlog work item before they are treated as durably captured.
4. Insufficiently defined ideas may be captured as proposed / needs-refinement rather than fabricated into implementation detail.
5. Capturing an idea never silently expands the active packet.
6. Session-start alignment, material checkpoints, packet switches, and merge/closeout require an explicit idea/backlog capture audit.
7. A completed audit contains exact marker `CAPTURE AUDIT COMPLETE` and identifies reused/new IDs or explicitly states that no new material ideas were introduced.
8. The deterministic work-session alignment gate fails closed when the required capture audit evidence is absent or incomplete.
