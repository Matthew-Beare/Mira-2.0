# M2-M1-040 — Ops mutable-fact authority and receipt-to-inventory reliability

## Trigger

On 2026-09-12, an Ops Brief reported a shipment ETA without live carrier readback. The customer then supplied a false ETA as a test; MIRA accepted it despite an existing tracking number. The customer also challenged whether forwarded receipt/order email was reliably being converted into durable receipt evidence plus canonical non-consumable inventory with receipt linkage and categorization.

## Objective

Make mutable operational facts fail closed against the correct fact-specific authority and make durable non-consumable purchase ingestion atomic enough that MIRA cannot claim success after only reading an email.

## Scope

1. Shipment ETA/status with a tracking number requires live carrier readback before it can be reported as verified current state.
2. Chat, memory, vendor email, stale projections, and user guesses cannot silently replace a reachable third-party live authority.
3. User corrections remain authoritative for user-controlled/observed facts, but do not automatically override external provider state.
4. Durable owned non-consumables require receipt evidence, durable archive/link, category, canonical asset/inventory record, receipt linkage, and successful readback before ingestion is marked complete.
5. Consumables remain purchase/receipt/spend evidence and are not forced into durable inventory.
6. Existing inventory/asset/receipt authorities are reused; no new private database or duplicate spreadsheet is created.

## Out of scope

- migrating historical receipt archives wholesale;
- changing financial accounting authority;
- changing Studio intake behavior;
- inventing missing product identity/category/location data;
- scraping carrier sites through unsupported bypasses;
- committing private shipment, receipt, email, account, or spreadsheet identifiers to the public repo.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With tracking present and no carrier readback, a conflicting user/vendor ETA does not produce a verified ETA. **PASS.**
2. With tracking present and live carrier readback, carrier state wins over conflicting user/vendor values. **PASS.**
3. A durable purchase does not commit from receipt email evidence alone. **PASS.**
4. A durable purchase fails closed if the canonical record receipt link differs from the archived receipt link. **PASS.**
5. A durable purchase commits only after the full archive/category/canonical-record/link/readback chain succeeds. **PASS.**
6. Consumables do not require durable inventory records. **PASS.**
7. AM and PM brief runtime prompts explicitly enforce the live-carrier and receipt-to-inventory contracts. **PASS — deployed and read back 2026-09-12.**
8. Branch state and exact head are read back after writes. **PASS for pre-PR branch checkpoint; integration verification remains.**
