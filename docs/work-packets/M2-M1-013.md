## Follow-up checkpoint — three-axis spending review and telemetry

The private live Financial Escape surface preserves spending intent on three independent user-controlled axes:

- **Purpose category** records what the economic spend was for.
- **Necessity** is independently selectable as `NECESSARY`, `UNNECESSARY`, or `UNKNOWN`.
- **Allowance** is independently selectable as `YES`, `NO`, or `UNKNOWN`; it represents deliberate permission, not merely budget inclusion.
- Positive economic-spend rows require category, necessity, allowance, and vendor resolution before becoming fully reviewed.
- Credits, transfers, repayments, and other non-spend rows remain explicitly non-spend rather than receiving fabricated lifestyle labels.
- Unknown historical values remain unknown until explicitly classified.
- Private spending analytics separate necessity, allowance, their intersections, review completeness, and related trend/detail views without exposing private transaction values publicly.
- `UNNECESSARY + YES` means deliberately permitted discretionary spend; `UNNECESSARY + NO` is the allowance-bending / unplanned-discretionary signal.
- The review surface is an input/view layer over stable canonical event identity, not a second financial authority.
