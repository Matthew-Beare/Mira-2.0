## Objective

Create one evidence/reconciliation model for Financial Escape so the command center can answer debt-exit questions without double counting purchases, ignoring returns, or allowing receipt/order data to masquerade as posted cash.

## Authority contract

- Posted connected-finance transactions/balances are authoritative for money actually moved.
- Email receipts, orders, shipping, refunds, returns and cancellations are evidence about purchase identity, purpose and reversal state.
- Uploaded historical mailbox/Amazon history is private evidence for backfill and reconciliation.
- User corrections outrank model inference.
- Financial Escape/Google Sheets are human projections and controls, not an independent money authority.
- One economic event is counted once. A receipt and its matching card transaction are not two expenses.
- Refunds/returns/cancellations must offset or reverse the matched purchase in net-spend views without deleting the historical gross purchase record.

## Work IDs

- `FIN-SOURCE-TRUTH-001` — authority/provenance/dedupe contract across provider transactions, receipts/orders and private projections.
- `FIN-RECEIPT-RECON-001` — idempotent receipt/order matching and item-purpose enrichment.
- `FIN-RETURN-RECON-001` — explicit return/refund/cancellation linkage and net-spend reversal semantics.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Uploaded mailbox export is parsed privately with stable fingerprints and no public-Git content leakage.
2. Purchase/refund/return/cancellation candidates are normalized and deduplicated by stable evidence identities.
3. Existing Amazon history is reconciled with mailbox evidence and provider transaction evidence where available.
4. Known returns/refunds are represented explicitly and cannot remain counted as ordinary net spending after a verified reversal.
5. Financial Escape reserve policy reflects the current user-confirmed holdout-month control only after private Sheet/provider readback.
6. Monthly whole-household spend views and category month-over-month metrics use posted money movement as the amount authority; receipt evidence improves classification only.
7. Actual debt-exit progress uses posted debt/cash changes only.
8. Readback verifies the private source/reconciliation surfaces before the packet closes.
