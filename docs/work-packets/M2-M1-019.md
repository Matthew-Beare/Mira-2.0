## User-visible outcome

MIRA can run People Discovery against active target companies and maintain a Google Sheet with `COMPANIES`, `PEOPLE`, `INTERACTIONS`, and integrated-or-bounded `JOBS` views. The system strongly prioritizes WGU plus networking/cloud/infrastructure people, avoids weak-volume stuffing, can explain why each person matters from stored evidence, and lets the user record manual contacts/replies/follow-ups in ordinary language.

## Hard product boundary

People Discovery may discover, enrich, rank, track, and draft later on explicit request. It must not automatically send email, LinkedIn requests/messages, comments, posts, or any other outreach. No LinkedIn scraping, login automation, or browser scraping is permitted.

## Provider direction at packet start

Use a provider abstraction. Current official-document reconnaissance indicates People Data Labs is the strongest first adapter candidate because Person Search can query arbitrary Person Schema fields including current employer, education/school, location, job title, skills, employment history, LinkedIn URL, and premium certification fields. Apollo remains a viable secondary adapter, but its documented People Search filters do not expose a direct school/education filter, making the WGU-first search less precise.

Provider selection remains evidence-based and replaceable. Secrets/credentials never enter public Git.

## Architecture constraints

- One logical MIRROR authority. The human-readable Google tracker is a projection/input surface over canonical person/company/interaction/job records, not an independent conflicting truth store.
- Provider facts retain provider/source ID or URL, observed time, confidence, and field status (`verified`, `inferred`, `stale`, `conflicting`, or `human_entered` as applicable).
- One real person maps to one canonical person record. Strong identifiers drive automatic merges; ambiguous identities remain separate/reviewable.
- Re-running discovery updates/enriches existing records rather than duplicating blindly.
- Discovery target volume defaults to quality-oriented approximately 5-15 useful people per active company but remains configurable.
- Private third-party people data and live spreadsheet/provider identifiers stay out of public Git.
