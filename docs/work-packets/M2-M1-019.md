## User-visible outcome

MIRA can run People Discovery against active target companies and maintain a Google Sheet with `COMPANIES`, `PEOPLE`, `INTERACTIONS`, and integrated-or-bounded `JOBS` views. The system strongly prioritizes WGU plus networking/cloud/infrastructure people, avoids weak-volume stuffing, explains why each person matters from stored evidence, and lets the user record or later reconcile manual contacts, replies and follow-ups in ordinary language.

MIRA must be able to answer from canonical state:

- Have I contacted this person?
- Which channel did I use?
- When was the last interaction?
- Did they reply?
- Am I waiting on them or do I owe a follow-up?
- Is there an active conversation or referral?
- Which target company/job was the interaction tied to?

## Hard product boundary

People Discovery may discover, enrich, rank, track and draft later on explicit request. It must not automatically send email, LinkedIn requests/messages, comments, posts or any other outreach. No LinkedIn scraping, login automation or browser scraping is permitted.

The canonical relationship tracker exposes observation/record/read operations only. Human-performed outbound activity may be recorded after it happens. Incoming replies may be recorded manually or later observed through an authorized provider path. Relationship status is derived from immutable/append-only interaction history rather than maintained as a second writable truth column.

## Provider direction

Use a provider abstraction. Official-document reconnaissance indicates People Data Labs is the strongest first adapter candidate because Person Search can query fields needed for WGU-first discovery including current employer, education/school, location, job title, skills, employment history and LinkedIn URL. Apollo remains a viable replaceable secondary adapter but is not a prerequisite for independent implementation.

The user does not need to connect both providers before engineering continues. Secrets/credentials never enter public Git. If a provider credential is still the only external boundary after implementation and CI, request the smallest single user action needed for the first live proof, expected to be one People Data Labs credential/account path unless later evidence changes the choice.

## Architecture constraints

- One logical MIRROR authority. The human-readable Google tracker is a projection/input surface over canonical person/company/interaction/job records, not an independent conflicting truth store.
- Provider facts retain provider/source ID or URL, observed time, confidence and field status (`verified`, `inferred`, `stale`, `conflicting`, or `human_entered` as applicable).
- One real person maps to one canonical person record. Strong identifiers drive automatic merges; ambiguous identities remain separate/reviewable.
- Re-running discovery updates/enriches existing records rather than duplicating blindly.
- Discovery target volume defaults to quality-oriented approximately 5-15 useful people per active company but remains configurable.
- Interaction history records channel, direction, kind, time, outcome, optional private summary/source reference, reply linkage and follow-up due date.
- Relationship state such as contacted, awaiting reply, replied, conversation and referral is derived from canonical history.
- Private third-party people/message data and live spreadsheet/provider identifiers stay out of public Git.
- Public tests and examples must use synthetic identities and synthetic message summaries only.

13. Interaction records can be associated with person, company and job identifiers and preserve channel/direction/time/outcome/reply linkage.
14. Automated outreach is absent and explicitly test-prohibited; no send/connect/post API is exposed by the tracker.
15. The tracker uses `COMPANIES`, `PEOPLE`, `INTERACTIONS` and `JOBS` projection surfaces without making the spreadsheet a second authority.
16. Relevant tests and baseline gates pass.
17. Durable public implementation/checkpoints contain only sanitized semantics and synthetic test data.
18. Remote Git/CI state is verified before completion is claimed.
